Our work, named U-GMo (Your Great Moment), comprises of three primary steps comprise the artificial intelligence part (AI Part) of our automatic video editing system: (1) posture detection, (2) graduation identification, and (3) video segment extraction. We utilize the YOLOv8 [2] object detection model for graduate detection, which has been pre-trained using data on human posture. In this version of YOLO, the YOLOv8 has faster inference speed and better accuracy, which is commonly used for many pieces of research about object detection [3-4]. Graduates use certain body language and gestures as they walk into a target area to accept their degrees duing the graduation ceremony. Using the YOLOv8 posture detection capabilities [5-6], we are able to localize the body of every graduate as they walk and get a degree in each video frame. The monitored graduation detections are then linked together using a posture detection. As a result, we are able to select a set of video frames that trace the movements of each graduate as they enter the target area and leave after being awarded their degree. Lastly, we use heuristic criteria and movement analysis to identify the important frames that capture the high point of each graduate's degree conferral, such as when they are standing in the middle of the stage and shaking hands with their diploma. This peak moment is the center of a little video clip that we take out to create the graduate's customized highlight reel.
