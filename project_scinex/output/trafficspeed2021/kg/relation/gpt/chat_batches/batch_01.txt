In this study we use the term "spatial" to refer to the prediction of the individual of a target km stone using the features from neighbor km stones which are near the location of the target and have high correlation in terms of speeds with high support. Besides, the term "temporal" refers to slicing data by the aspects of times, which are hour durations, weekday groups, and prediction in next N minutes. According to the results, using spatial-temporal models for traffic speed prediction provides the better performance than the baseline . It is because the traffic speed of every reference point has a pattern. Some km stones might have similar speed in every day, some might have similar pattern in an interval time in every day, some might have similar pattern among working days and holidays, and some have dependency pattern among nearby km stones. Our approach attempts to attract these associations of speeds of each km stones among different time periods on the basis of linear associations.
