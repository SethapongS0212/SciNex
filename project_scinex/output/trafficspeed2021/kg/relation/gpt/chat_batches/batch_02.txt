This paper demonstrates an approach to the prediction of speed at km stones using spatial and temporal aspects. Our work is one important task of building a model for travel time prediction in Thailand . This study makes prediction models based on the linear regression technique by the view of using speeds of each km stone as time series data. The experiment has done by four mains steps. First, GPS transaction and km stones information are mapped into time series data of speeds at each km stone. Second, more information of any date times is extracted to be hour durations and weekday groups. Third, the time series dataset is sliced into chunks of data in the same hour duration, and the same weekday group. Then, the Pearson correlation among all milestones with lag of 5, 10, 15, and 20 minutes are computed, and the dependency networks among km stones are constructed by selecting any pairs of km stones having high correlation and support. Last, in the fourth step, linear models of all target km stones under the spatial- temporal conditions were built. The overall accuracy is 9.23 in terms of RMSE processed by 5-fold cross-validation, and it better than the regression model without spatial-temporal aspects about 30.14%. It can be viewed that our proposed method can be applied in an application for monitoring traffic status and becomes a pathway to be a model for travel time prediction. However, not all km stones satisfy the linear behavior.
