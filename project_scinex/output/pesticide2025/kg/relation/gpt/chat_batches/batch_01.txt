Section: 2.2. Low-cost portable spectrophotometer

Text:
A low-cost, portable spectrophotometer was designed to enable the utilization of smartphone cameras for capturing spectral images . These images were subsequently processed using a combination of image processing techniques and machine learning algorithms. The design ensured that each image contained two spectra. The first was the reference spectrum, generated when light passed through a void. This spectrum served as a reference, compensating for any inclinations, movements, or shifts in light intensity that may occur during the experiment. The second was the analysed spectrum, produced when light traversed a solution, altering its intensity before it reached the camera sensor. This design facilitated a comprehensive and accurate analysis of light intensity changes. A schematic representation of the spectrophotometer is depicted in Fig. For assembly, the diagram should be printed and adhered to a corrugated plastic sheet, followed by cutting the sheet along the solid line. Part number 1 is affixed to one side of part number 2, which is folded to form a box. Subsequently, part number 3, equipped with a 50 µm slit, is attached to the opposite side.
