These oxidation reactions, including oxide formations and volatilization, would be more aggressive when exposed to higher partial pressures of oxygen (cO2), higher temperatures (the features T and Tin) and longer oxidation times (t)4,5,58 resulting in the thickening of the oxide layer promoting the stress in the oxides and thus the spallation tends to occur5. The chemical compositions of alloy included in the top-10 features were Cr, C and Si. For Cr, it played the major role in promoting the oxidation resistance of the alloys. Higher Cr contents increased oxidation protection performance61,62. Meanwhile, C had a detrimental effect on the oxidation resistance and resulted in a porous oxide layer63 leading to easier spallation compared to the dense oxides. Si was found to be oxidized by O2 and usually forms SiO2 located at the metal/ oxide interface64. It was demonstrated that the addition of Si suppressed the formation of iron oxides and decelerated the oxidation of all alloys65–67.

Section: Conclusions

Text:
This work proposes a novel method for predicting spallation during high- temperature oxidation processes of alloys, utilizing machine learning. After collecting raw data from the literatures, several machine learning models were trained and tested. The 15-estimator Random Forest (RF) proved to be the best predictive model, with the top 10 features being "(Cr,Mn,Fe)3O4", "Si", "Cr2O3", "Ti", "C", "t", "Cr", "T", "cAr", and "cO2". When the model was tested with unseen data from the evaluation based on 3-fold cross-validation, the accuracy and F1 score were approximately 88.26% and 87.13%, respectively, while predictions from PBR yielded an accuracy of only 41.86%. Therefore, this machine learning model demonstrated a significant advantage over conventional PBR calculations for spallation predictions. Based on the use of the random forest technique for spallation prediction, this work can be considered as a part of the demonstration that machine learning can play an important role in the field of material sciences.
